## Placement policy

- Generated photos use controlled crop-to-frame placement.
- Legacy diagrams use contain/letterbox placement at their native aspect ratio.
- No imported image is independently stretched in width or height.
- Image placement measurements are recorded in `build/image_aspect_report.json`.
